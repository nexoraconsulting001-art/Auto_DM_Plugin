Your account took years to build. Automation shouldn't put it at risk.

Bhava is designed to reduce the risk of bans and shadowbans. Here is exactly how, and what no tool can promise.

What Bhava will never do

  • Ask for your Instagram password
  • Scrape Instagram or run bots on your account
  • Send DMs to people who never contacted you
  • Auto-follow, auto-like or buy engagement
  • Sell your data or your customers' data

The rules are built into the product

Instagram and Facebook messages all pass through one safety check. If a message breaks a rule, Bhava doesn't send it, and you can see why.

Official API only

Bhava connects through Instagram's official login. You can disconnect it at any time from your Instagram settings.

One reply per comment

Instagram allows one private reply to a comment, within 7 days. Bhava keeps to that and never tries again on the same comment.

The 24-hour window

Once someone messages you, Instagram gives you 24 hours to reply. Bhava won't send outside it.

A queue with a speed limit

Replies wait in a queue, spaced out for each account. When Meta signals a limit, Bhava backs off, even if a reel goes viral.

Limits per person

Nobody gets repeated DMs from the same automation, however many times they comment.

Stop means stop

If someone says stop, they get no more automated messages from you.

Complaints reach a person

Comments about scams, refunds or missing orders are never answered by a bot. They go to you.

Public replies are off

Identical public replies under every comment look like spam, so they are off unless you turn them on.

Reach Guard watches your account

Reach Guard is a health score for your account. It tracks your reach and any warnings from Meta, tells you early when something changes, and slows sending down by itself.

Every business on Bhava is kept separate, and each has its own off switch. A problem on one account can be stopped without touching anyone else's.

What no tool can promise

Meta decides what happens to accounts on its platforms, and it changes its rules. Accounts can be restricted for reasons that have nothing to do with automation, such as the content you post, reports from other people or a mistake in automated review.

So we don't promise that your account will never be restricted, and you should be wary of anyone who does. We build to the rules, show you the risk, and act on it early.
