If you connected an Instagram or Facebook account

Remove Bhava from your account. We are told automatically and start deleting.

  • Instagram: open Settings, find Apps and websites, then remove Bhava.
  • Facebook: open Settings, find Apps and websites, then remove Bhava.

We then delete your access token, stop your automations and delete your data from Meta's platforms within 30 days. You will get a confirmation code and a link to check progress.

If you have a Bhava account

Use "Delete my account" in Settings, in the web app or the Bhava for Business app.

If you commented on or messaged a business using Bhava

Email us with your Instagram or Facebook username and the business's name. Comment and message text is automatically deleted after 30 days in any case.

If you bought from a shop on Bhava

Ask that shop, or use "Delete my account" in its app or site. If you can't reach the shop, email us with the phone number on your order.

What we have to keep

The law requires us to keep some records for a set time, such as tax invoices. Safety and compliance records are kept for 13 months, then deleted.
