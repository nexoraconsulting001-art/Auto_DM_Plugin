This policy explains how {{company.legalName}} ("we") handles personal data for Bhava, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We never sell personal data.

This website

This website uses no tracking or advertising cookies. Everything on it, including its fonts, is served from our own server, so no other company sees your visit. Our servers keep basic logs, such as IP addresses and pages requested, to keep the site secure. If you contact us, we use your message and details only to reply.

Early access requests

If you ask for early access, we collect your name, your phone number or email, your Instagram handle and, if you tell us, what you sell, along with your consent and when you gave it. We use these details only to contact you about early access to Bhava. We keep them until early access opens to you or you withdraw your consent, whichever comes first. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us, and we will delete your details.

If you choose to send your request on WhatsApp or by email instead, it reaches us through WhatsApp or your email provider, under their own privacy policies.

What Bhava collects

  • Sellers and creators: name, phone number, email, business details, billing details and login activity.
  • Connected Instagram and Facebook accounts: account ID, username, access tokens (stored encrypted), posts, reels, stories, insights, and the comments and messages that automations respond to.
  • People who comment or message a connected account: their platform ID, username, and the comment or message.
  • Shoppers: name, phone number, email, delivery address, orders, and how they used a shop's pages.

Why we use it

  • To run the features you asked for: automated replies, shop pages, orders, payments, shipping and insights.
  • To keep accounts safe, follow platform rules and prevent fraud.
  • To send service messages, such as login codes and order updates.
  • To meet our legal obligations, such as tax records.

We use data from Meta's platforms only to provide Bhava to the business that connected its account. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell or license it.

Who handles shoppers' data

Each shop on Bhava decides why and how its customers' data is used, and we process that data on the shop's behalf. Businesses on Bhava cannot see each other's orders or customers.

If a shopper agrees to use one Bhava sign-in across shops, we are responsible for that shared identity: verified phone or Google account, saved addresses and an anonymised cash-on-delivery risk level. A shopper is asked for consent first and can withdraw it at any time.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to run Bhava, and only what they need:

  • Meta, for Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp messages
  • Payment gateways, such as Razorpay or Cashfree
  • Shipping partners, such as Shiprocket
  • Cloud hosting, storage and email providers
  • AI providers, only if a business turns on AI replies
  • Authorities, where the law requires it

How long we keep it

  • Raw notifications from Meta: 7 days
  • Comment and message text, with usernames: 30 days
  • The record of why a DM was or wasn't sent: 90 days
  • Account health history: 90 days
  • Safety and compliance records: 13 months
  • Orders and invoices: as long as tax law requires

When a business leaves Bhava or disconnects its account, its data from Meta's platforms is deleted within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a complaint. Here is how to delete your data. If you bought from a shop on Bhava, you can also ask that shop directly.

Children

Bhava is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly process children's data without verifiable parental consent.

Security

We encrypt access tokens and payment keys, keep each business's data separate and limit who on our team can reach it. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell active users.
