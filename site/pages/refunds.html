Cancelling a plan

You can cancel at any time from your account. Your plan keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, then moves to the free plan or closes. You won't be charged again.

Monthly and yearly plans

Plan fees are for access to the service, so we don't refund a period that has already started, including the unused part of a yearly plan. We will refund you where the law requires it.

Billing mistakes

If we charged you twice or charged the wrong amount, tell us within 30 days at {{company.supportEmail}}. We will correct it and refund the difference to your original payment method, normally within 7 working days of confirming the mistake.

Setup services

If you bought done-for-you setup, you get a full refund if you cancel before we start. Once work has started, the fee is not refundable.

Charges from others

Payment gateway fees, shipping, WhatsApp and AI usage are paid to other companies and passed on at cost. We can't refund them once they are used.

Buying from a shop on Bhava

Each shop sets its own returns and refunds. If you bought something, contact the shop first; its details are on your order page. Refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank or UPI app may take a few more days to show them.
