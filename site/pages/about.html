In every Indian market, a sale starts with the same question.

Kya bhaav hai? What's the price?

Why it's called Bhava

Bhaav means feeling. It also means price. When a reel makes someone feel something, they ask what it costs, and that moment is where a sale begins.

Today that moment happens in the comments. Most of those questions go unanswered, because no seller can reply to all of them in time.

What we're building

Bhava answers those comments in DM, sends people to a shop in the seller's own brand, and shows which reels actually sold. It is for creators and small sellers across India, and it grows with them from a free plan to their own store and apps.

We build it around Instagram's rules from the start, because a seller's account is their business. Read how.
