Questions

What is the free creator page?

One page for your shop, or for you as a creator, at its own address. It has shelves of your reels and products and a search box, and you can put it in your Instagram bio or list it as your website. On the free plan it shows a small "Made with Bhava" badge at the foot of the page. Paid plans remove the badge, and the page shows only your name and logo. See an example.

Does Bhava take a cut of my sales?

No. The only sales-related costs are what others charge: payment gateway fees, Shiprocket and any WhatsApp or AI usage. We pass these on at cost.

Do I need a website?

No. If you have one, Bhava reads your look from it. If you don't, start from one of five looks and add your logo.

Can I use my own Razorpay or Shiprocket?

Yes. Payments and shipping can run on Bhava's accounts or on your own.

Who pays for Google Play and App Store accounts?

You do. Your apps are published under your own developer accounts, so they stay yours.

What happens if I run out of DMs?

You can add 10,000 more for ₹299 or move up a plan.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. See refunds and cancellation.
